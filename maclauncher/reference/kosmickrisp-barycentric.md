# Fragment shader barycentrics

MacLauncher KosmicKrisp exposes `VK_KHR_fragment_shader_barycentric` on Metal devices supporting shader barycentric coordinates. The extension provides perspective and non-perspective barycentric weights and the original per-vertex values of fragment inputs.

Pixel-center weights on independent list primitives use native Metal barycentrics. Per-vertex inputs, simultaneous interpolation modes, centroid and sample evaluation, strips, fans and reordered provoking vertices use primitive-corner emulation. The rasterization vertex shader maps each corner to the original vertex or index, preserves ordinary vertex side effects, and supplies the requested values through flat varyings. A heap buffer carries per-vertex values when the varying interface has insufficient space. Full interfaces also move selected ordinary inputs through this per-corner storage, preserving their original interpolation or flat provoking value. This frees native slots for the basis interpolants without reducing the exposed input limits. Centroid and sample weights use Metal interpolation methods at their requested evaluation locations.

Native inputs and the primitive-corner basis use Metal interpolant operations at the requested centroid, sample or pixel-relative offset. Perspective and non-perspective offset evaluation retain their original control flow, including partially covered pixels whose pixel-center reciprocal-W is zero. Independently compiled fragment stages with explicit interpolation compile at final link. Basis-only producers keep the original vertex body without corner-body copies; flat-only producers evaluate the provoking corner once. Per-vertex transport evaluates all required corners.

Indexed, indirect and primitive-restart draws use the same draw conversion and bounds-checked index handling. Geometry shaders retain their complete adjacency input and emit their normal Poly output; barycentric emulation operates on the rasterization stage after that output. First-vertex and last-vertex provoking modes preserve flat inputs. Triangle-strip barycentric vertex order follows the selected provoking mode, so `triStripVertexOrderIndependentOfProvokingVertex` is false.

The extension adds no corner cloning, index conversion, additional pass, heap allocation or fragment load to shaders without barycentric or per-vertex inputs. Their existing per-draw upload size is preserved. The separate [full-interface fix](kosmickrisp-varyings.md) packs interfaces that exceed the native varying budget.

The capability is implemented by the [barycentric adaptation](../../src/kosmickrisp/) in the [ordered registry](../../src/kosmickrisp/). Its per-vertex lowering is adapted from [Mesa merge request 45585](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/merge_requests/45585), commits `f5eaaf815a96c9d0ab166f3d426f6a45e7f4e47b` through `97397f07948bef4286bd822ae90dce23c51a3cd8`, by Sascha Scandella, under Mesa's MIT license.
